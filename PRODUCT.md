# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React. The implementation should stay lightweight and fast enough for a classroom demonstration.

## Users

Primary users are players and community members who need to find and reserve sports facilities quickly. Secondary users are Manggahan Sports Complex staff who review and manage reservations.

## Product Purpose

Manggahan Active is a responsive reservation management system for discovering facilities, checking schedules, making reservations, and managing bookings across several sports disciplines. Success means a player can complete a reservation confidently while staff can immediately understand the resulting schedule.

## Positioning

The player booking flow and the staff schedule share one clear visual system, so a reservation moves visibly from discovery to confirmed facility schedule without feeling like two disconnected products.

## Operating Context

Players may book from a phone or desktop before visiting the sports complex. Staff use the management view to see daily occupancy and reservation status. The graded classroom demonstration includes a short pitch limited to two minutes, and the interface design is the main voting criterion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Discover facilities by sport, date, and time.
- Compare community league standings across every supported sport.
- View venue details, amenities, capacity, operating hours, and available time slots.
- Enter player and contact details, review a reservation, and confirm it.
- View, reschedule, or cancel upcoming reservations.
- Give staff a lightweight dashboard for daily reservations and status management.
- Provide separate demo sign-in paths for players and administrators; these credentials are front-end classroom data, not production authentication.
- Use connected interactive components rather than disconnected static screens.
- Keep the first implementation focused on the player reservation flow, followed by a simplified management dashboard.
- Pricing, payment processing, authentication, and production backend behavior remain open decisions and must not be presented as finished capabilities.

## Brand Commitments

The approved working name is Manggahan Active. The product should feel modern, energetic, friendly, and appropriate for a community sports facility.

## Evidence on Hand

The assignment brief is `/Users/mcclave/Downloads/Situational Activity 2.0 HCI.pdf`. No official logo, photography, facility inventory, customer evidence, pricing, or production data has been provided; demonstrations must use clearly fictional sample content.

## Product Principles

- Make availability understandable at a glance.
- Keep one obvious next action on every screen.
- Preserve user choices while moving forward or backward through booking.
- Use familiar booking patterns with strong feedback and accessible controls.
- Let the player flow remain simple while keeping staff status visible and actionable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The responsive web interface should support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, reduced motion, semantic form labels, and touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels.
